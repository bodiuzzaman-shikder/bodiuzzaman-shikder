## 👋 About Me

I'm an innovative electrical and mechanical design engineer with hands-on experience across **power systems, robotics, automation and advanced manufacturing**. At **Adex Engineering Ltd.**, I design HT (11–33 kV) and LT switchgear, instrument transformers and power transformers (0.15–28 MVA), combining SOLIDWORKS modelling with MATLAB-based engineering calculation and FEA/CFD validation.

I hold **five professional SOLIDWORKS certifications** (including **SOLIDWORKS Champion**) and have represented Bangladesh at international competitions such as the **University Rover Challenge** in Utah, USA. I'm currently seeking **funded graduate research opportunities** at the intersection of **mechatronics, robotics and sustainable energy**.
